## Purpose and completed capability

Prodmap is a local, evidence-bounded side project for correlating runtime,
telemetry, deployment, and timeline context into conservative analytical views.
It does not make causal, health, commercial, or product-thesis claims.

The completed scope includes local provenance and runtime inventory, offline
OTLP ingestion and observed topology, deployment-ledger context, baseline and
conservative regression views, agent-ready investigations, portable sanitized
investigation packages, and the read-only stdio MCP server `prodmap-mcp/v2`.
MCP v2 exposes exactly two tools: `list_deployments` and
`investigate_deployment`.

## Safety and compatibility review

- Analytical SQLite access is read-only and uses consistent snapshots.
- MCP discovery and investigation remain bounded, redacted, and do not claim
  causality.
- Existing migrations are exactly `000001` through `000005`.
- Versioned JSON contracts retain non-null arrays and sanitized output.
- Baseline, regression, and classification preserve explicit uncertainty:
  `UNKNOWN` is not `LOW`; `CANDIDATE` is not confirmed; `NO_SIGNAL` is not a
  health claim.

## Remaining limitations and decision status

- No broad corpus establishes accuracy, recall, or threshold calibration.
- The evidence model does not prove causal relationships or healthy state.
- Raw source access, generic MCP query/history tools, writes, network sources,
  scoring, and commercial validation remain out of scope.
- The commercial thesis and a formal `go`, `pivot`, or `stop` decision remain
  inconclusive.
